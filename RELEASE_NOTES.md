# Namma Krishi Prices — Release Notes

A clearer, smarter way to discover daily commodity prices across Karnataka.

## What's included

- Browse **147 commodities** across **177 markets**, with **369 varieties** covered in the current data snapshot.
- Data is collected from **6 official sources**.
- Search by commodity, market, or variety and quickly find relevant price cards.
- Results are grouped by market alphabetically, with the freshest updates shown first within each market.
- Commodity cards show prices, grade, arrivals, units, source, update dates, and freshness indicators.
- Tap **See Price History** to view historical price trends, including actual and carried-forward prices.
- Share any commodity price card using a direct, shareable link.
- Improved market naming and normalization for a cleaner browsing experience.
- English and Kannada interface support.
